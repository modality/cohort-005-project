## Office hours overview

Along with the self-paced material that ships with this course, there are also live office hours with me.

Each session is run as a live stream on YouTube, as an unlisted video. You don't need any special equipment to join in, and you don't need any logins. You just go to the page.

The video stays on that page afterwards. If you're worried about missing an office hour, don't worry. Go to the link, and if the session has already happened, the recording will be there.

## Schedule

There are six office hour sessions during the course, and each is 45 minutes long.

## Optional attendance

These sessions are completely optional. You can finish the entire course without attending a single one.

They're mostly just for vibes, for answering questions, and for exploring things that are outside the remit of the course. They're absolutely not mandatory.

## Finding the links

The links and the exact times for every session are on the lessons page, below this video.

Go on YouTube and add the little bell icon, or something similar, so you get the alert and don't miss out.

## Asking questions in the Discord

Any question you want answered in the office hours goes in the Cohort 005 office hours channel in the [Discord](https://www.aihero.dev/discord).

Create a new post there: give it a title, write your question, and post it.

## Voting on questions

In the office hours, I have a page that pulls in your questions live. Crucially, you can vote on other people's questions as you go.

To vote, add an emoji reaction to a question. Each unique person who reacts counts as one vote, and the votes take the most popular questions to the top.

Once I answer a question in the session, it gets marked as answered in the Discord.

If your question is similar to one someone has already asked, search for their post first. You might find a good discussion going on there.

This lets you take part in the office hours completely asynchronously if you can't attend. If there's a question you really want answered, put it in the forum.

## Urgent questions

I only dip into the office hours channel during the office hours themselves. If your question is more urgent, put it in the Cohort 005 questions channel in the [Discord](https://www.aihero.dev/discord) instead. That's the main place for questions.

The office hours are for the things you want to see me talk through, and for interesting discussions with other people.

## Wrap up

Those are the office hours. Check out the links below the video, and click the bell so you don't miss them.

Nice work, and I'll see you there.
